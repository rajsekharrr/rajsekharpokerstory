## What Is This?

This is not a resume. This is a poker table.

Six players sit around a felt table, each one holding a chapter of my life.
Click a player. Press **BET** to hear their story.
Press **FOLD** and find out why that button doesn't work for me.

Built entirely with voice — every prompt spoken into
[Wispr Flow](https://wispr.flow) and built on [Emergent](https://emergent.sh)
as part of the **Wispr Flow × Emergent Hiring Challenge 2026.**

## About Me

I'm **Rajsekhar Roy** — final-year B.Tech CSE (AI/ML) student at VIT Chennai,
graduating July 2027.

I've been taking bets my whole life:

- Traded stocks in Class 9. Lost money. Kept thinking about markets.
- Went all-in on JEE in Delhi. Burned out. Landed at VIT.
- Entered college at 104.5 kgs. Left the first year with a friend group,
  a gambling story, and a lesson in bankroll management.
- Did full-stack at Tribal Wolf (gen-Z fashion startup at VIT)
  and AI internship at Velocis Systems.
- Built **Toqsy** — an app to help people cold approach others in real life
  and reduce social anxiety.
- Building **Freeze Mail** — a browser extension for cold emailing founders,
  because I've sent hundreds of them and felt the pain.
- Cold emailed a marketing head. Got the internship at Quadrafort.
- Got placed at an MNC. Not taking it. Want a startup.
- Playing poker. Finally in profit.

I don't fold. I adjust my bet.
